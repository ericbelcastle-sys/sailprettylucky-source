Terms & Cancellation

The essentials for chartering aboard Pretty Lucky. Your charter agreement is the governing document — this page summarizes it in plain language.

Booking & Reservation

A week is held for you once Wendy confirms availability and the charter agreement is signed. Inquiries are handled personally — we reply within 24 hours.

Payment Schedule

  • 50% deposit reserves your week.
  • Remaining 50% due 60 days before boarding.

Cancellation & Refund

  • 60+ days before boarding: deposit refunded less a 10% administrative fee.
  • 30–59 days: deposit is retained but may be applied (one time) to a rescheduled week within the same season, subject to availability.
  • Under 30 days: payments are non-refundable; trip insurance is strongly recommended.
  • If Pretty Lucky cannot deliver the charter (mechanical or weather), you receive a full refund or a pro-rated reschedule — your choice.

Included in the Rate

  • Boat, captain + mate, all standard meals and standard bar.
  • Fuel for the agreed itinerary, water toys, snorkel gear, linens and housekeeping.

Not Included

  • Applicable BVI & USVI customs/immigration, park permits and cruising taxes.
  • Premium provisions and specialty requests, crew gratuity (customary 15–20%), and dockage/mooring fees outside the itinerary.

Travel Insurance

Strongly recommended for all guests. We can point you to providers when you book.

Contact

Questions on any of this? Email sailprettylucky@gmail.com — Wendy replies within 24 hours.
